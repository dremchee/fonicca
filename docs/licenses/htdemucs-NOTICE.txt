Single standard HTDemucs network, not the four-network fine-tuned ensemble.
FL Studio's installed engine refers to Demucs, but the exact architecture
version and checkpoint used by FL Studio have not been identified.

Fonicca framing follows demucs.apply.apply_model and TensorChunk: centred
padding with adjacent track context, positive triangular overlap-add,
25% overlap, no random shifts. Whole-track normalization is removed after
inference; DC is not reintroduced into every stem, matching Fonicca's other
normalized separation pipeline. All four stems share one inference per window.
